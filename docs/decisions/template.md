# NNNN. Title in the form of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Task:** 2.2 (or "none" for a decision outside the plan)

## Context

What forces the decision, what is already known, and what constrains it. Link to notes, papers or earlier decisions.

## Options

1. **Option A.** What it is, what it gains and what it costs.
2. **Option B.** ...

## Decision

What was chosen and why it beats the alternatives. Keep it to one paragraph.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier or harder.
- Any change to the plan: tasks added, dropped, split or moved. The same PR updates the README.
- What the thesis has to state, and where.
